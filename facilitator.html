Welcome to the Gambia FLP Teacher Portal

This portal gives teachers and trainers early access to the Teacher Guides for ECD 2, ECD 3 and Grade 1 in all eight languages of instruction, the Grade 1 numeracy guide for the seven national languages, and the facilitator guides used in teacher training.

It is a temporary measure, developed by the CREDD–MoBSE Material Development Team to support teacher training and early access to the materials. It remains available pending the completion, by the MoBSE EdTech team, of the full integration and transition of the FLP hub to a dedicated page on the MoBSE EdTech hub.

Using it for the first time. Choose your subject, language and class at the top of the guide. The eight languages are English, Jola, Mandinka, Manjaku, Pulaar, Seereer, Soninke and Wolof. The facilitator guides are for trainers running training sessions; the teacher guides are for classroom use.

Using it without internet. After your first full visit while connected, most of the guide is saved on your device and opens without a connection. The first visit downloads the guide for the language you choose, so do it where the connection is good. For the safest offline copy, add the portal to your home screen: on Android, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen; on iPhone, do this in Safari — tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Audio plays only for a recording you have already opened once while connected. Connect from time to time so that corrected content reaches you.

Attribution

The portal and the material within it are the official property of the Government of The Gambia, developed under the Gambia FLP of the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education.

Feedback

If a guide will not open, a lesson is missing or incorrect, or you wish to report anything else about the portal, use the feedback form: Report a problem or send feedback. The form asks which guide and which page you were on, so that the fault can be traced. Please do not include the names of learners, or any personal details about a child, in the form.
